## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. Is ShareX completely free for commercial and personal use?

Yes. ShareX is released under the open-source GPL license, meaning it can be used for both personal and commercial work at no cost.

#### 2. Does ShareX require an external server account to function?

No. You can use ShareX strictly as a local screen capture and recording utility by saving screenshots directly to your local storage folders without connecting any online accounts.

#### 3. How do I record MP4 screen videos with audio in ShareX?

Navigate to **Task Settings > Screen Recorder**, click **Screen recording options**, and select **Download** to automatically acquire the FFmpeg library. You can then choose your microphone and system audio sources under the audio recorder options.
